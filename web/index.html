Which setup is yours?

Laptop & laptop

Install the Duet extension on both browsers. It hooks into whatever video is on screen — Netflix, Prime, YouTube, Disney+, Max, Jellyfin, a local file — and keeps both sides in step. Nothing to configure per service.

Laptop & TV — best result

Run an HDMI cable from the TV person's laptop to the TV, or cast the browser tab. The extension does the syncing; the TV is just a bigger monitor. Every service works, because the browser is still the player.

Laptop & native TV app

A Netflix app on a smart TV can't be driven from outside — no app allows it. Open TV mode there instead and Duet runs a cue console: a shared 3-2-1 countdown, matching timecode, and a plain instruction like skip back 4 seconds when you drift. You press the buttons; Duet tells you exactly when.

Duet never copies, relays, or re-streams video. Both of you need your own access to whatever you're watching.
