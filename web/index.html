How it works

One shared moment. Three simple steps.

Duet works with what you already watch. Your video never passes through us.

  1. 01

    Open what you already watch

    Each person streams from their own account in Chrome. Duet never copies or relays the movie.

  2. 02

    Pair your screens

    Use two laptops, a laptop and TV, or two TVs with the companion controls.

  3. 03

    Stay on the same frame

    Play, pause, seek, and countdown cues keep both sides together down to a fraction of a second.
