How it works

One shared moment. Three simple steps.

Duet works with what you already watch. Your video never passes through us.

  1. 01

    Open what you already watch

    Each person streams from their own account in Chrome. Duet never copies or relays the movie.

  2. 02

    Pair your screens

    Use two laptops, a laptop and TV, or two TVs with the companion controls.

  3. 03

    Stay on the same frame

    Play, pause, seek, and countdown cues keep both sides together down to a fraction of a second.

      Advanced TV setup details
      

        
Native TV app
Use TV mode as a cue console with shared countdowns, matching timecode, and simple drift instructions.

        
Fire TV / Android TV
Run the local Fire TV helper on a computer connected to the same Wi-Fi network as the TV.

        
Roku
Run the Roku helper on the same network. It joins your room and relays transport controls.

        
Nebula Capsule
Use the Capsule helper after opening and pausing the title in the native Netflix app.
